## No license required

SimDrive is not offered as a commercial product (INIT-2026-605). There are
no paid tiers, no license keys to activate, and nothing to renew — every
tool is available immediately after `pip install simdrive`, on any number
of machines, including CI runners.

If you were previously running a paid tier, no action is needed — the full
tool surface is available without a key.
